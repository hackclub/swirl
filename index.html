Who

Every Hack Clubber

Swirl is open to all Hack Clubbers! All teenagers 18 and under, anywhere in the world can participate!

What

Your own website

Swirl is a part of Hack Club's web-dev series! It's your time to shine and push yourself further than ever before. You will get a food grant between $5 and $9 by shipping your own unique project, dependent on how many features you add! See the requirements page for more information.

How

Research, then build

Cool features can be found all over the internet, it is encouraged for you to do research on specific attributes you may want to add. Using ai, copy-pasting templates, and claiming other's code as your own makes your submission ineligible for a grant. There is a guide dedicated to Swirl, and it is recommended that you check it out!
